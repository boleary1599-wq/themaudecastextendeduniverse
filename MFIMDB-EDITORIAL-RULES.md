Independently establish each episode's title, duration, participants, video ID and transcript paths. Never copy episode-specific facts into another episode.

## 2. Exhaustive research
Read the entire transcript, then perform a second dedicated lore pass. Include introductions, tangents, interruptions, jokes, asides, advertisements, songs and closing exchanges. Extract ALL recoverable facts and obsessive micro-details: named puppets, pets, programmes, objects, places, incidental characters, exact relationships, numbers, habits, phrases, staff hierarchy, ambitions, possessions, previous adventures, disagreements and institutional rules. Early episodes are especially important. Distinguish references, hypothetical jokes and comparisons from established events. Retain unclear names for audio verification. Compare with all preceding ledgers, record first appearances and developments with internal timestamps, and flag contradictions. Never invent missing details or use later-episode spoilers. Follow the Continuity Bible for evidence management.

## 3. Required editorial features
Use the six sections in the Design Bible's locked order.
- Museum Story: The Story So Far: The Maudecast Universe. Neutral third-person unfolding narrative about the institution, its people, relationships, motivations, adventures and traditions. Thematic narrative subheadings and cumulative continuity ledger.
- Spectral Speculations: separate Maude-universe narrative and cumulative ledger. Lead with established Maudecast mythology, narrating it as this universe's history, then integrate relevant Simpsons background. No repeated non-canon disclaimers or references to hosts inventing it. Preserve genuine uncertainty; do not promote rejected hypotheses into established events.
- Both stories: no public timestamps, critical commentary, editorial process notes or references to characters before their evidenced introduction. Preserve source classifications internally.
- Literary Essay: see section 4.
- Mauditorium: structured cumulative factual register about Maude, with source timestamps and provenance. Distinguish verified Simpsons facts, recollections, interpretations and unresolved claims; do not present invented biography as verified Simpsons facts. Verify external facts separately if expanding beyond the episode.
- Sponsor: document real and fictional advertisements, distinguish confirmed sponsorship from jokes, say when none is identified. Never invent sponsorship.
Preserve recurring features such as Maude Art and The World Without Maude when present.

## 4. Literary criticism
Approximately 800 words (normally 750–900), distinctive title, a few thematic headings only where they advance the argument. Original standalone cultural criticism in the spirit of The Quietus. Episode 1's The Importance of Being Nobody is the exemplar. The complete transcript is the intellectual backbone. Develop its ideas across segments, not a segment-by-segment recap or checklist. Never refer to hosts, brothers, speakers or conversational dynamics; analyse Maude, mythology, cultural objects and arguments directly. Popular culture dominates: sitcoms, cartoons, comedy, pop music, genre cinema, toys, advertising and everyday ephemera. Occasional highbrow references to philosophy, literature, art or serious cinema should genuinely illuminate the argument. Draw references from inside and outside the episode. Avoid gratuitous name-dropping, invented quotations, unsupported influence claims and academic stuffiness.

## 5. Transcript — mandatory
Every distinct speaking turn must have a named speaker. Use audio and context; where uncertain make the best contextual guess and mark it provisional in metadata/notes. Never use Unknown, Unidentified or blank speaker labels. Episode 1 speakers are Brendan and Nathan unless evidence establishes others; identify speakers independently for subsequent episodes.
Merge adjacent same-speaker blocks in stored data and display; show the name only when the speaker changes. Preserve all dialogue in original order, including jokes and digressions, and retain timed cues within merged turns. Correct obvious recognition errors supported by context, without inventing speech. Re-merge after attribution corrections. Provide original TXT and speaker-labelled JSON, clickable timestamps seeking the embedded video, optional compact cue display, counts and load-error fallback. Be explicit about provisional attribution and unverified audio.

## 6. Publication gate
Before declaring an episode complete: confirm original video ID and embed; verify complete named transcript with no adjacent repeated speaker headings; test timestamp navigation; complete two lore passes; reconcile cumulative ledgers and internal evidence; check all six panels, essay voice and fact provenance; verify links and responsive layout. A page missing required elements is an explicitly incomplete draft, even if committed to GitHub. Do not describe a commit as a verified live deployment without checking. Preserve existing work and update the Bibles when new standards are agreed.

See [Continuity Bible](MFIMDB-CONTINUITY-GUIDE.md) and [Design Bible](DESIGN_CHEATSHEET.md).

## Cumulative recap versus current episode
In each of Museum Story and Spectral Speculations, separate The Story So Far (prior episodes only, maximum approximately 300 words per universe) from This Episode (unrestricted length; exhaustive current developments and micro-details). Episode 1 needs no prior recap. Keep the compact cumulative continuity ledger separately, without a word cap. Never sacrifice current episode evidence to meet the recap limit.

## Transcript reading mode — latest rule
Supersedes earlier visible timestamp requirements: display one clickable checkpoint approximately every five minutes (up to ten minutes where natural). Do not display line-by-line timestamps. Join consecutive caption fragments into readable prose within each named speaker turn. Show speaker names only when the speaker changes. Preserve every original cue and precise timestamp in the underlying TXT/JSON archive for research and future navigation. Do not discard dialogue.
