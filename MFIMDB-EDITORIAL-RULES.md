# MFIMDb editorial and transcript rules

## Mandatory speaker attribution — ALWAYS

Every dialogue turn in every published Maudecast transcript **must** have a speaker name. Never leave a turn unlabelled, and never use “Unknown”, “Unidentified”, or blank labels as a substitute.

1. Use the source video/audio and context to identify the speaker wherever possible.
2. If the speaker cannot be determined confidently, **make the best contextual guess** and still print a name.
3. Record uncertain assignments as provisional in editing metadata or notes, so they can be corrected later; don't imply that guesses have been verified.
4. Merge consecutive lines from the same speaker into a readable turn.
5. Include timestamps, ideally clickable links that seek the embedded YouTube video.
6. Preserve the dialogue, including jokes and digressions; correct obvious speech-recognition errors such as “mod funders” → “Maude Flanders” when supported by context.
7. For Episode 1, the hosts are Brendan and Nathan. Do not add other speakers without evidence.
8. YouTube video/audio is the primary source. Whisper transcripts are drafts requiring attribution and proofreading.

**Acceptance criterion:** No episode transcript is considered publication-ready until 100% of turns have a named speaker, including provisional best guesses.

## Continuous speaker turns — mandatory display rule

**Show the speaker name only when the speaker changes.** If Brendan speaks in three successive transcript blocks, merge them into **one Brendan turn**; do not print Brendan three times. The same applies to Nathan and all future speakers. A new named turn begins only when another person speaks.

- Combine adjacent blocks by the same speaker in both the **stored transcript data** and the **published page**.
- Preserve all spoken words in their original order. Never delete dialogue just to merge blocks.
- Preserve original timestamp cues inside a merged turn so readers can still click through to the corresponding YouTube moments. Display the speaker heading once, followed by timestamped passages as needed.
- If a later correction changes a speaker assignment, re-merge neighbouring turns so no two adjacent turns have the same speaker name.
- A speaker label is still mandatory on every **distinct speaker turn**; it does not need to repeat on every subtitle or paragraph.

## The Story So Far — mandatory episode feature

Every Maudecast episode should have a **The Story So Far** feature alongside its embedded YouTube video and attributed transcript. Unlike the FALLOUT podcast's critical essays, this is an evolving **character and plot continuity archive**.

- Explain **what happens to, or is revealed about, relevant characters in the current episode**, and place those developments within the series/podcast continuity **up to that episode only**.
- Track relationships, character motivations, changes, callbacks, unresolved questions, and notable plot developments; include a compact cumulative **continuity ledger** that can be carried forward and updated.
- Clearly distinguish **established Simpsons canon**, **the hosts' recollections or interpretations**, and **Maudecast-invented speculative lore**. Never silently promote a guess, joke or imagined backstory into canonical fact.
- Record named characters invented in podcast segments as **MFIMDb speculative characters** and track them in later instalments when they recur.
- Base each instalment primarily on the episode's original YouTube audio and transcript; verify external Simpsons facts separately if expanding beyond what the episode actually says.
- Make the feature readable as a narrative with character-focused sections, not a transcript recap or generic review. Avoid spoilers from later podcast episodes when writing the current episode's running record.

## Continuity management

Before publishing each new **The Story So Far**, follow the [MFIMDb Continuity Guide](MFIMDB-CONTINUITY-GUIDE.md), update the character and plot ledgers, and record contradictions instead of silently rewriting established history.

## Mandatory episode architecture — two universes and recurring features
Every episode contains its title, embedded YouTube video, metadata, and the following editorial features:
1. **The Story So Far: The Maudecast Universe** — the Museum, Theme Park and Database as an institution and fictional setting; Brendan, Nathan, Stamp and other staff/characters when introduced; their relationships, adventures, running jokes and institutional developments.
2. **The Story So Far: Maude's Universe** — Maude's life and relationships, Simpsons canon discussed, and the imagined biography emerging through Spectral Speculations. Keep verified canon, host recollection and invented lore distinct.
3. **The Literary Essay** — a short original critical essay about the episode's comedy, ideas, themes and form, grounded in the episode rather than a plot recap.
4. **The Mauditorium** — a cumulative fact archive about Maude. Label verified facts versus host assertions, interpretations and unresolved claims; include source timestamps.
5. **The Sponsor** — document sponsors and advertising segments, distinguishing real sponsorship from fictional or joking advertisements. If none is identified, say so; never invent one.
6. **Full Transcript** — preserve all existing mandatory transcript rules.
Preserve additional recurring segments such as Maude Art and The World Without Maude when present. Do not introduce characters or developments before their first evidenced appearance.

## Literary essay standard
Target approximately 800 words (roughly 750–900), original highbrow-meets-lowbrow cultural criticism. Use specific episode details as the basis for an argument, with plentiful relevant references across literature, television, comedy, music, film and popular culture. References must illuminate the episode, not merely name-drop. Avoid generic recap, invented quotations and unsupported claims of influence. Use a distinctive title and thematic sections. The two Story So Far strands must be independent sections with separate continuity ledgers.
