## Flandball
**Canonical spelling:** Flandball

A fictional game/competition within the Maudecast Museum universe, invoked in the disputed story of how Nathan came to own the Museum and the staff's intensive training. The Episode 8 title is **The Flandball Inheritance**.

**Never substitute:** Flungball, flung ball, or other automatic-caption guesses when the game is intended.
